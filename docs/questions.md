Only real questions that need your answer. Each one ends with a question mark and gives two sentences of context, the options, and what I will do if you do not answer. Decisions already made live in docs/decisions-log.md; plan changes live in docs/progress.md.

## Open questions

1. **Box selection:** dragging left to right (encloses) and right to left (crosses) are what you expect from 2D drawing programs, and you like the colours. What else behaved wrongly: which kinds of items got selected, what was selected after the drag ended, or something else? Until you say, nothing changes in box selection.

2. **Measure during a drag (the M key):** while dragging a tool, pressing M lets you pick a measurement on the model (for example an edge parallel to the drag) and then type its value to set where the face ends up. It has no button, so you may never have seen it. Should it (a) stay as it is, (b) get an on-screen button during a drag, or (c) be removed? Until you say, it stays as it is.
